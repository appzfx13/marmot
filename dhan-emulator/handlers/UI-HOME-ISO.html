FAQ

Common questions before you start

Do I need to sign or connect a wallet to start?
No wallet connection or signature is required to start in read-only mode via Telegram. You only sign when you decide to execute or approve automated actions.
Is this safe? Does Ava hold my funds?
Ava Protocol is entirely non-custodial. Your funds always remain in your personal wallet under your keys. Ava can only operate within the precise constraints and limits you approve.
What can Ava actually do?
Ava monitors health factors, tracks portfolio positions across protocols like Aave, executes risk management strategies, and handles multi-step on-chain workflows seamlessly.
What does it cost?
Getting started and monitoring your wallet positions is free. Transaction fees apply only when executing on-chain automation workflows.
Do I need to know DeFi or how to code?
Not at all. You can interact with Ava using plain natural language right inside Telegram without writing any code or dealing with complex setup procedures.
Which chains and protocols?
Ava supports major EVM-compatible chains and top-tier lending/DeFi protocols like Aave, ensuring comprehensive portfolio coverage.

Not sure where to start?

Tell Ava what you're worried about. It reads your wallet, watches for risk, and pings you in Telegram when something needs attention.
